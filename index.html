Private Offline AI

Privacy Policy

Private Offline AI has no account, no analytics and no server. The AI model is inside the app and runs on your iPhone, so your conversations are never sent anywhere by default.

Last updated: 18 September 2026.

The short version.

We do not operate a server, and we receive nothing from the app — not your messages, not your documents, not usage statistics, not even a crash report. A few features can reach the internet, and every one of them is off until you switch it on. They are listed below, and none of them send anything to us.

What we collect

Nothing. The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party SDKs of any kind. There is no account to create and no identifier assigned to you.

What the app stores, and where

Everything lives in the app's own storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS data protection. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

WhatWhere
Conversations and chat historyThe app's Documents folder
Notes, to-dos, transcriptsDocuments folder and iOS app preferences
Saved images and generated gamesThe app's Documents folder
AI models you import (.gguf)The app's Documents folder
Settings — theme colour, light/dark, instructionsiOS app preferences
API keys and mail credentials you enteriOS app preferences (see below)

The app's Documents folder is visible to you under On My iPhone → Private Offline AI in the Files app. That is deliberate, so you can get your own data out. It also means anything with access to your unlocked device and the Files app can read it.

Credentials

If you connect a cloud AI provider or an email account, the key or password you enter is stored in the app's private preferences on your device, protected by iOS data protection. It is used only to talk to that provider directly. It is never sent to us and never shared with anyone else. For email we recommend using an app-specific password rather than your main account password, and revoking it from your provider if you stop using the feature.

When the app uses the internet

The core experience — chatting with the built-in model, reading your documents and images, notes, to-dos and transcription — works with the device in Airplane Mode. These are the only paths that leave your phone, and each one is started by you:

FeatureWhat is sent, and to whom
Web search (the globe button) Your search terms go to DuckDuckGo, Bing or Google to fetch results. Your conversation is not sent.
In-app browser Ordinary web browsing. The sites you visit see what any browser would show them.
AI Gallery image search Your image search terms go to Bing.
Mail The app connects directly to your provider over IMAP and SMTP using the credentials you entered. Nothing passes through us.
A cloud AI provider you add yourself If you switch from the on-device model to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Groq, Mistral, DeepSeek, Qwen or a custom endpoint, that conversation is sent to that provider under your own key and their privacy policy applies.

The on-device model is the default. Nothing above happens unless you turn it on, and turning it off stops it.

Permissions the app asks for

PermissionWhy

Each is requested the first time you use the feature, and the app works without granting any of them.

Children

The app is a general-audience product and is not directed at children under 13. Because we collect nothing, we hold no information about anyone, including children.

Your rights

There is no data of yours for us to hand over, correct or delete, because we never receive any. Your own copy is in the app and in the Files app, and you can export or delete it yourself at any time. Deleting the app removes everything.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app sends anything anywhere new, this page and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version ships.
